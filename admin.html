Admin Instructions

Use the Google Sheet below to manage your products and categories. Changes made in the sheet will automatically reflect in the store.

  • Products Sheet: Manage all your products with details like name, category, price, stock, etc.
  • Categories Sheet: Manage product categories and their display names.
  • Orders Sheet: View and manage customer orders.
  • Make sure to keep the column structure exactly as shown below.
  • Click "Refresh Data" to immediately update the store with your changes.

Google Sheets Management

Edit your products and categories directly in the Google Sheet below:

Google Sheet Structure

Products Sheet Columns:

  • A: ID (Number) - Unique identifier
  • B: Name (Text) - Product name
  • C: Category (Text) - Category ID
  • D: Price (Number) - Regular price
  • E: Discount Price (Number) - Optional discount price
  • F: Image URL (Text) - Product image URL
  • G: Stock (Number) - Available quantity
  • H: Description (Text) - Product description
  • I: Seasonal (TRUE/FALSE) - Is seasonal product

Categories Sheet Columns:

  • A: ID (Text) - Category ID (e.g., "fruits")
  • B: Name (Text) - Display name (e.g., "Fruits & Vegetables")
  • C: Product Count (Number) - Number of products in category
